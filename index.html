I am a junior at The Hotchkiss School in Lakeville, Connecticut, and a pianist in the Precollege Division of the Manhattan School of Music, where I study piano with Professor Delana Thomsen and chamber music with Professor Claudia Knafo, and sing in the Senior Select Choir.

I began the piano at the age of seven with David Brooks and gave my first solo recital in 2024. I have won First and Second Prizes at the Golden Key, Vivo, Musical Life, and Charleston International competitions, and have performed at Carnegie Hall's Weill Recital Hall and the Kaufman Music Center's Merkin Concert Hall — with an upcoming performance alongside the Warsaw Philharmonic.

Away from the keyboard, I bring together technology and the arts: I built a conductor's-baton-like electronic instrument and I'm developing an AI-powered music tool. I'm also drawn to mathematics and science, and I race alpine skis and play racquet sports.
