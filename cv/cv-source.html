Projects

The Baton Instrument
Created through Creativitas at MIT: a conductor's-baton-like electronic instrument using four buttons, scroll wheels, and gyroscopes to control music through a synthesizer; premiered in concert.
Auralis
Independent AI-powered piano transcription and practice project that converts recordings into MIDI and readable notation. Developed with feedback from professors, startup CEOs, and students at Stanford and Carnegie Mellon.
